## Full description
AnimaStage Lite by FBNonaMe is a portrait MMD (MikuMikuDance) studio for your phone.

Load a folder or ZIP with PMX/PMD models, textures, and optional VMD motion. Preview in 3D with WebGL, play animations, edit morphs and bones on the timeline, apply bloom FX, and export MP4 video.

Features:
• Import PMX/PMD + textures (PNG, TGA, BMP) from ZIP or folder
• VMD motion playback with optional camera motion
• Timeline with keyframes, curves, and animation templates
• Morph and bone controls
• Bullet physics (WASM) when supported on device
• Portrait fullscreen interface optimized for phones
• MP4 export via system Share (Gallery, Files, Drive)
• Client-side only — files stay on your device, no account

User-provided content only — you supply your own models and motions. The app does not include copyrighted characters or music.

Requirements: Android 6.0+ (API 23), OpenGL ES 3.0 recommended.
