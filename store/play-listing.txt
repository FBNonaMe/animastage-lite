## Full description
AnimaStage Lite is a full MikuMikuDance-style studio on your phone — not a limited demo.

Load PMX, PMD, or GLB/FBX characters, play VMD motion, grade the look with MMD Fidelity and custom LUTs, frame shots in Camera Studio, and export MP4 for Shorts. Everything runs on your device. No account required.

Features:
• PMX / PMD / VMD import (drag & drop or folder)
• GLB / FBX import for Blender and Sketchfab assets
• MMD Fidelity — classic toon shading and outline
• Custom LUT (.cube / .3dl) and Bloom ↔ Grade order
• DDS / BMP textures for older MMD packs
• Timeline, mocap from video → keyframes
• Camera Studio / Virtual Cinematography tools
• 9:16 Shorts and landscape MP4 export
• Portrait-first studio on Android (Scene, Control, Camera, FX)

Your models stay on your device. Client-side only.

## Release notes v1.3.0

What's new in this release:

• MMD Fidelity render mode — classic toon shading plus outline for a desktop-MMD look
• Custom LUT import (.cube / .3dl) with strength control; drag LUT onto the viewport
• Effect stack order — Bloom before color grade/LUT, or grade before Bloom (modoki-style)
• DDS and BMP texture loading for older MMD models (fewer broken materials)
• Improved vertical 9:16 framing — camera pulls back so characters fit the frame
• Stronger GLB/FBX support: materials and lighting, mocap bone mapping for generic skeletons
• Video mocap → timeline keys (body tracks: head, arms, spine, hips)
• Cinematography Studio (VCS) — director modes, shots, lighting, camera tools
• Landing and product positioning: full studio on Web and Android (not a demo)
• Stability fixes: GLB morph/template crash, Canvas eventSource, bloom defaults, import lighting

Bug fixes and polish:
• Over-bright / white GLB materials reduced
• Bloom and exposure defaults for cinematic and anime looks
• Safer texture repair when PMX points to missing TGA but BMP/DDS/PNG exist
• Portrait format no longer crops characters as tightly
