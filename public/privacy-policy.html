Privacy Policy

AnimaStage Lite · Developer: FBNonaMe
Last updated: June 1, 2026

Applies to: animastage-lite.app (website) and Android app (com.webmmd.suite)

This Privacy Policy explains how AnimaStage Lite (“we”, “the app”, “the website”) handles information when you use our website at animastage-lite.app or our Android application on Google Play.

Summary

  • We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data.
  • PMX, VMD, and other files you open are processed locally on your device.
  • No account or registration is required.
  • No advertising SDKs or analytics trackers in the current version.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect:

  • name, email address, or phone number;
  • precise or approximate location;
  • contacts, photos, or media library (except files you explicitly select);
  • device identifiers for advertising or tracking;
  • payment information (the app is free).

Website (animastage-lite.app)

The website runs as a client-side web application in your browser. When you load models or motions, processing happens in your browser memory. We do not operate a server that receives your model files.

Standard web hosting (e.g. Vercel) may process technical connection data (IP address, user agent) for security and delivery. We do not use this data to identify you or build profiles.

Android app

The Android app uses a local WebView to run the same studio interface.

  • INTERNET permission — required for the WebView runtime. Your model files are not uploaded to our servers.
  • File access — you choose PMX/PMD/VMD/ZIP files via the system file picker. Files are read locally only.
  • MP4 export — saved through the Android Share menu or device storage. You choose the destination (Gallery, Files, cloud apps).

Third-party content

You may load your own MMD models, motions, music, and textures. You are responsible for copyright and usage rights. AnimaStage Lite is a tool only and does not distribute third-party copyrighted content.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use of the website or app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
